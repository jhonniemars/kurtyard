More Than Just a Court — It's a Community

KURTYARD is your neighborhood pickleball destination, built for players of all levels. Set against a backdrop of swaying palms and tropical greenery, our courts offer a unique playing experience that combines sport with serenity.

Whether you're dropping in for a casual rally, hosting a friendly tournament, or looking to improve your game, KURTYARD has everything you need. Our well-maintained courts, quality equipment, and welcoming atmosphere make every visit a great one.
